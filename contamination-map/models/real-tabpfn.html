Notes

TabPFN v2 further pre-trained on 71 real datasets from OpenML and Kaggle. Deduplication was only against its own evaluation suite (29 AMLB datasets with <10,000 samples), not against TabArena: 'We exclude any dataset from the pre-training data that does not meet these criteria.' The paper says the weights are public on Hugging Face but does not name the checkpoint file. Several training datasets match TabArena datasets by name (Amazon employee access, Online Shopper's Intention, Bank marketing).
