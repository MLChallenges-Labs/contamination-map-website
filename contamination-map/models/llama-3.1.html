Notes

'The Llama 3 Herd of Models' covers Llama 3.1 ('All results in this paper are for the Llama 3.1 models.'). Contamination was measured against the pre-training corpus using 8-gram overlap (Singh et al., 2024). Other Table 15 benchmarks not in the shared id list (AGIEval 98%, BIG-Bench Hard 95%, BoolQ 96%, QuaC 99%, WorldSense 73%, SiQA 63%, PiQA 55%, NaturalQuestions 52%, CommonSenseQA 30%, OpenBookQA 21%, SQuAD 0%) are also reported. The paper states 'We do not include any training sets from commonly used benchmarks in our annealing data.' (training sets, not test sets). The authors separately tested annealing on GSM8K/MATH training sets as an experiment.
