Notes

Pre-trained only on real OpenML data (self-supervised targets), no synthetic data. The paper deduplicated training data against its own evaluation suites CC18 and CTR23 ('Any dataset pairs with unusual similarities were manually evaluated and those found to be related were removed from training.'), not against TabArena. Seven training datasets overlap TabArena-v0.1 per TabArena's own analysis. The paper does not say whether v1.0 and v1.1 used the same data; the TabDPT-Turbo paper reports 112 datasets for v1.1 vs 123 in Table B.1 (see open questions).
