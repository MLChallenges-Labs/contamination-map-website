Notes

Paper's 'TiRex-2-fev' column (Tables 4-7). Notably includes four GIFT-Eval test datasets (bitbrains_fast_storage, bitbrains_rnd, bizitobs_application, bizitobs_service), BOOM data (filtered for leakage), and Chronos electricity_15min / monash_electricity_hourly / monash_traffic / exchange_rate (long-horizon source data); not meant for GIFT-Eval evaluation. Plus ~15M synthetic GP series and 1% dysts trajectories. Other stated exclusions, not tracked here: fev-bench (all).
