Notes

Frozen OpenCLIP ViT-B/16+ (LAION-400M) with a trained adapter and residual head. Trained on the whole MVTec AD dataset (train + test) and evaluated on the other eight datasets (VisA, ELPV, SDD, AITEX, BrainMRI, HeadCT, MNIST, CIFAR-10). Few-shot normal prompts at inference are sampled from the target dataset's training set. A third-party paper (AdaptCLIP) notes InCTRL provides separate official models for different shot numbers (2/4/8).
