Notes

The image encoder is initialised from MAE pre-trained Hiera, and the SAM 2 paper does not state that MAE's pre-training data. Open-source VOS data (DAVIS, MOSE, YouTubeVOS) appears only in a data-mix ablation, not in the released model. The paper v2 says its results are based on SAM 2.1 and does not give separate training data for 2.1, so one entry covers both.
