DocVQA

50,000 extractive questions on 12,767 scanned document images (industry documents from the UCSF Industry Documents Library, dated 1900-2018, mostly 1960-2000); questions and answers written by annotators. Random 80/10/10 split: train 39,463 questions, validation 5,349, test 5,188. Metric ANLS. Test answers are not in public copies (lmms-lab HF copy has answers: null for test; lmms-eval scores docvqa_test with a 'submission' metric for the RRC evaluation server); reports cite either test (server) or validation.
