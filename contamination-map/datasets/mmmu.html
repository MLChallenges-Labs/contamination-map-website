MMMU (Massive Multi-discipline Multimodal Understanding and Reasoning)

11.5K multimodal college-level questions (multiple-choice and open) collected by ~50 college students from textbooks, online sources and lecture materials, across 6 disciplines, 30 subjects, 183 subfields and 30+ image types. Splits: dev 150 (5 per subject, few-shot), validation 900, test 10,500. The validation set (900) is what most reports use. Test answers were withheld behind an EvalAI server at release (Nov 2023), but the authors released them on 2026-02-12, so all splits now have public answers.
