Vision / Dataset

Caltech-101

Pictures of objects in 101 categories (about 40 to 800 per category, most about 50; roughly 300x200 pixels), plus a background category. No official test split: CLIP and CLIP_benchmark/DataComp use the VTAB split (`vtab/caltech101`, 6,085 test images, 102 classes including background), scored with mean per-class accuracy.
