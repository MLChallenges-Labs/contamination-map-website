Vision / Dataset

GTSRB

Single-image, multi-class traffic-sign classification: more than 50,000 images of 43 classes; each physical traffic sign occurs only once in the dataset. CLIP Table 9 lists 26,640 training and 12,630 test images; the 12,630-image test set is evaluated.
