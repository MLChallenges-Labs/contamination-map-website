CharXiv

2,323 real charts from arXiv preprints (Jan 2020 - Sep 2023, eight subjects), each with 4 descriptive questions and 1 reasoning question written and verified by human experts (10K+ questions). Split: validation 1,000 charts (QA pairs public) and test 1,323 charts (answers kept private; null in the public data). Reported 'CharXiv reasoning/descriptive' scores are normally on the validation split.

Also known as
CharXiv, CharXiv (reasoning), CharXiv-R, CharXiv reasoning, CharXiv (descriptive), CharXiv-D, charxiv_val_reasoning, princeton-nlp/CharXiv
