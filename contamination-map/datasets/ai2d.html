AI2D (AI2 Diagrams)

Over 5,000 grade-school science diagrams (scraped from Google Image Search with seed terms from Grade 1-6 science textbook chapter titles) with rich annotations and over 15,000 multiple-choice questions written via Amazon Mechanical Turk. The paper used a 4,000-image train / 1,000-image 'blind test set' split, but the public download (ai2d-all.zip, v1.0.0, Sept 2016) contains the correct answer for every question. LMM evaluations use a 3,088-question test split (lmms-lab/ai2d, used by lmms-eval).

Also known as
AI2D, AI2 Diagrams, AI2D (test), AI2D-Test, ai2d, lmms-lab/ai2d
Domain
grade-school science diagram question answering
