Electricity (ECL, 321 clients)

Hourly electricity consumption of 321 clients, 2012-2014 (26,304 hourly steps), built by LSTNet from UCI ElectricityLoadDiagrams20112014 by dropping 2011 and clients with zero values; the long-horizon 'Electricity' benchmark dataset.

(2017) with 321 hourly series 2012-2014 (Zenodo 4656140; length 26,304 on forecastingdata.org, same as DLinear Table 1). Identity is inferred from these matching descriptions. The bare name 'Electricity' is ambiguous and not an alias.
