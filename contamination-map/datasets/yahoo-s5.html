Yahoo S5 Labeled Anomaly Detection Dataset (Yahoo Webscope S5)

Labelled anomaly detection dataset released by Yahoo Labs via the Webscope programme: 367 univariate time series in four benchmarks A1-A4 (A1 series named e.g. 'A1-Real1'), partly based on real traffic to Yahoo services (anomalies labelled manually by editors) and partly synthetic (anomalies generated algorithmically). No official train/test split.
