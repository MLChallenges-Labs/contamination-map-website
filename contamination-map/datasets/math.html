MATH (Mathematics Aptitude Test of Heuristics)

12,500 competition mathematics problems (AMC 10, AMC 12, AIME and others) with step-by-step solutions, split into 7,500 training and 5,000 test problems, 7 subjects, difficulty levels 1-5. The paper notes many of the problems can be collected from aops.com.

Notes

The original HF copy hendrycks/competition_math is disabled ('DMCA Takedown notice'); the official README now points to the mirror qwedsacf/competition_math. Problems come from older public competitions (AoPS), so they predate the dataset by years. Copies found in public web data by third-party studies: Llama 1 pre-training corpus: Substantial contamination percentages for MATH in the Llama 1 pre-training corpus; significant estimated performance gain seen for Llama 1 models.; MATHInstruct: 769 MATH test problems (15.4%) have rephrased counterparts in MATHInstruct (Table 7).
