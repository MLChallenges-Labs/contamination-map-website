MMMU-Pro

Harder re-filtering of MMMU: questions answerable by text-only LLMs were removed, candidate options were augmented from 4 to 10, and a 'vision' setting embeds each question in a screenshot/photo. 1,730 questions, each in 'standard (10 options)' and 'vision' form (3,460 total), plus a 'standard (4 options)' config; on HF all configs have a single 'test' split of 1,730 with the answer field included (answers public). 577 of the questions come from the MMMU validation set (the rest from other MMMU questions).
