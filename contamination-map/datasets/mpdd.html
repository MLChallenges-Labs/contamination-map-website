Vision / Dataset

MPDD

Dataset for visual defect detection in industrial (painted) metal parts manufacturing: more than 1000 images with pixel-precise defect annotation masks, divided into a training subset with anomaly-free samples and a validation (test) subset with normal and anomalous samples.

Also known as
Metal Parts Defect Detection Dataset, Metal Parts Defect Detection
