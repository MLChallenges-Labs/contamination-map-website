Vision / Dataset

KolektorSDD2

Surface-defect dataset of color images (about 230 x 630 pixels) of defective production items from Kolektor Group: 356 images with visible defects and 2979 without. Train set: 246 positive and 2085 negative images; test set: 110 positive and 894 negative images; defects annotated with fine-grained segmentation masks. Note: unlike the other datasets, the official train split contains defective images; anomaly-detection papers typically train on its negatives only (convention, not stated by the dataset authors).
