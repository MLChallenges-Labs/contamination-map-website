MathVista

6,141 examples drawn from 28 existing multimodal datasets (9 math-targeted QA, 19 VQA; up to 400 examples each) plus 3 newly created datasets (IQTest, FunctionQA, PaperQA; 736 new examples). Splits: testmini 1,000 (answers public; the split almost all reports use) and test 5,141 (answer labels not publicly released; leaderboard submission by email).

Also known as
MathVista, MathVista (testmini), MathVista-Mini, MathVista testmini, mathvista_testmini, AI4Math/MathVista
Domain
mathematical reasoning in visual contexts
License
CC BY-SA 4.0 for the new contributions; copyright of images and questions stays with the original sources; the card says it may be used as a test set but not as a training set

Notes

Most items come from older public datasets (several with public train/val/test files), so published_from is null. Each source is capped at 400 examples: 'Each source dataset is limited to up to 400 examples to ensure a balanced representation of each source in our final compiled benchmark.' (paper §2). A 2024 study found that at least 6.6% of MathVista questions use images from academic training sets often used in SFT (see open_questions).
