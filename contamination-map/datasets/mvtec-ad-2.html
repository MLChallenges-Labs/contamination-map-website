Vision / Dataset

MVTec AD 2

Eight anomaly detection scenarios (Can, Fabric, Fruit Jelly, Rice, Sheet Metal, Vial, Wall Plugs, Walnuts) with 8,004 high-resolution images in total. Training and validation sets contain only defect-free images (training captured under regular lighting). The test data is split into a public test set (TESTpub) with pixel-precise ground truth and two private test sets (TESTpriv, TESTpriv,mix) whose images are public but whose ground truth is held on an evaluation server; test images include several lighting conditions.
