Vision / Dataset

ObjectNet

Bias-controlled test set of 50,000 photographs of 313 object classes, taken by Mechanical Turk workers in their own homes with controlled rotations, viewpoints and backgrounds; 113 classes overlap ImageNet. No training set. Zero-shot ImageNet-style evaluation (CLIP_benchmark, DataComp) uses the 18,574 images of the 113 ImageNet-overlapping classes.

Notes

Unlike the other sets in this grouping, the photographs were taken for the dataset (the CLIP paper's overlap analysis calls ObjectNet 'novel data from after the date our dataset was created'). Its first public release date is not documented: the paper is in NeurIPS 2019 and the earliest web-archive capture of the site and download page is 2019-12-10, so published_from is left empty (TODO(verify)). The licence forbids training on it; copies posted online should carry a 1-pixel red border.
