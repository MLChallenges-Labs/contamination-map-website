Vision / Dataset

MVTec AD

Industrial anomaly detection dataset of 5354 high-resolution color images in 15 categories (5 textures: carpet, grid, leather, tile, wood; 10 objects: bottle, cable, capsule, hazelnut, metal nut, pill, screw, toothbrush, transistor, zipper). 3629 defect-free images for training and validation; 1725 test images (467 defect-free, 1258 defective) covering 73 defect types, with pixel-precise ground-truth regions for all anomalies (1888 regions).
