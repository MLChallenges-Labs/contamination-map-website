Notes

Pretraining used only the train split of each of the 20 pretraining datasets (Appendix B); the split is 'as defined in the dataset' (GluonTS/Monash-style), not stated to be the Informer long-horizon split. ETTh1/ETTh2/ETTm1 and the Monash versions of Electricity (321 clients) and Traffic (862 series) were in pretraining; ETTm2, Weather (Monash, Australian), Exchange Rate, Pedestrian Counts, Beijing PM2.5 and two Huawei cloud series were held out as unseen. No synthetic data. No newer Lag-Llama release found as of 2026-10-15. Other stated exclusions, not tracked here: Pedestrian Counts (all); Weather (all); Beijing PM2.5 (all); Requests Minute (all); Platform Delay Minute (all).
