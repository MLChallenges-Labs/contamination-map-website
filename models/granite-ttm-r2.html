Notes

Branches without the r2.1 suffix. Model card: pre-training data 'differs slightly' from the paper. The listed datasets are a subset of paper Table 8 (enterprise release drops LOOP_SEATTLE, SZ_TAXI, Q-TRAFFIC; also kaggle_web_traffic and nn5_daily from Table 8 do not appear in the r2 list). The paper's Weather/Electricity/Traffic/ETT exclusion is stated for the paper's pretraining set, not restated in this card. GIFT-Eval leaderboard configs for TTM-R2-Pretrained/Finetuned mark "testdata_leakage": "Yes" (the leaderboard notebook used TTM-r2 models from this repo, possibly r2.1 branches). Other stated exclusions, not tracked here: Q-TRAFFIC (all).
