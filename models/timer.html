Notes

Timer-1B/16B/28B are names for pre-training data scale (time points), not parameter counts. Checkpoints are distributed via Google Drive/Baidu links in scripts/README; which variant(s) they correspond to is not stated. The downstream-exclusion statement covers ETT, ECL, Traffic, Weather and PEMS, yet the zero-shot appendix says 'PEMS datasets are not included, as they have already appeared in the LOSTA dataset for pre-training' -- i.e. PEMS is in Timer-28B's data. The released HF checkpoint thuml/timer-base-84m belongs to the Timer-XL entry. Other stated exclusions, not tracked here: PEMS (all).
