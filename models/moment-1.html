    • UCR Time Series Classification Archive (2018) · split: train of ts-classification

      The HF repo folder classification/UCR/ holds 158 dataset folders = the 128 UCR 2018 univariate datasets plus all 30 UEA datasets, each with both _TRAIN.ts and _TEST.ts files. MOMENT states it adheres to the creators' predefined splits and pre-trains only on training splits, i.e. the official UCR TRAIN files. The entry covers two registry datasets (ucr-archive and uea-archive), hence two updates for this raw_name. MOMENT then evaluates classification on the TEST splits of 91 UCR datasets (unsupervised representations + SVM).

      Evidence
    • UEA Multivariate Time Series Classification Archive (2018) · split: train of ts-classification

      All 30 UEA 2018 multivariate datasets (ArticularyWordRecognition ... UWaveGestureLibrary, incl. EigenWorms, InsectWingbeat, MotorImagery, PEMS-SF) are present as folders under classification/UCR/ with _TRAIN.ts and _TEST.ts files (checked against the HF file listing). Only the TRAIN files are used per the paper; MOMENT is univariate, so channels are presumably treated separately (not stated).

      Evidence
    • UCR Time Series Anomaly Archive (Hexagon ML/UCR, KDD Cup 2021) · split: train of ts-anomaly

      TSB-UAD's 'KDD21' folder holds all 250 series of the UCR/KDD Cup 2021 anomaly archive (files 001-250). Each file name encodes the creators' training-end index (e.g. 35000); MOMENT says it uses creators' splits and only training splits for pre-training, so the anomaly-free training prefix of every series was seen in pre-training, not the test part. Caveat: the pre-training data pipeline is not public; the released fine-tuning/eval loader (momentfm) uses exactly this file-name split. MOMENT reports anomaly detection on subsets (44 / 248 series) of this archive.

      Evidence
    • Server Machine Dataset (SMD) · split: test

      IMPORTANT: the 281 TSB-UAD SMD files are single channels taken from SMD's machine-*.test.csv files (file names 'machine-X-Y.test.csv@k.out'), i.e. they come from the official SMD TEST period (the only labelled part). MOMENT pre-trains on the training portion of each such series (horizontal split; whether TSB-UAD-provided or its own 60/10/30 split is not stated), so a prefix of the official SMD test data is in pre-training. Univariate per-channel versions, not the 38-dim multivariate SMD. TimeRCD (third party) excluded MOMENT results on SMD for this reason. TSB-UAD (used here) is the 2022 predecessor of TSB-AD; TSB-AD-U/-M were curated from many of the same sources (e.g. YAHOO, NAB, UCR, MSL, SMAP, SMD, IOPS, MGAB), so overlap with tsb-ad-u/tsb-ad-m series is likely but not established by the sources cited here.

    • Yahoo S5 Labeled Anomaly Detection Dataset (Yahoo Webscope S5) · split: train of ts-anomaly

      367 YAHOO files (A1real 67 + A2synthetic 100 + A3/A4Benchmark 200) = all of Yahoo S5. Yahoo has no creator train/test split, so per MOMENT the series are split horizontally 60/10/30 and the first ~60% (training part) of every series is in pre-training; that portion contains labelled anomalies used by benchmarks that score the whole series (e.g. TSB-AD). TSB-UAD (used here) is the 2022 predecessor of TSB-AD; TSB-AD-U/-M were curated from many of the same sources (e.g. YAHOO, NAB, UCR, MSL, SMAP, SMD, IOPS, MGAB), so overlap with tsb-ad-u/tsb-ad-m series is likely but not established by the sources cited here.
