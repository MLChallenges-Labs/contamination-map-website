Notes

Instruct variants (SFT on Tulu 2 mix, DPO on cleaned UltraFeedback) are recorded as fine_tuning on the same entry; they are described in the same paper (v3/v4). TruthfulQA prompts were removed from UltraFeedback only (the Tulu 2 paper found UltraFeedback used TruthfulQA prompts); the Tulu 2 SFT mixture itself is not stated to be decontaminated. Dolma v1.5 contains >90% of HumanEval test problems per the Dolma paper (see corpus dolma-1.5). Later OLMo 1.x releases (OLMo-7B-0424 / 1.7 on Dolma 1.7, OLMo-7B-0724) are not covered.
