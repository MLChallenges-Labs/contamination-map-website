Contamination analyses

Analyses that found benchmark test data in this model's training data, or in a corpus it uses.

  • MATH (Mathematics Aptitude Test of Heuristics) · pre-training data

    Via ProofPile2 (Llemma paper): ~7% of MATH test problem statements and 0.6% of MATH test solutions have 30-gram hits in Proof-Pile-2 (OpenWebMath/AlgebraicStack). CONDA 2024 report also lists hendrycks/competition_math and gsm8k as contaminated in EleutherAI/proof-pile-2.

    Evidence
  • GSM8K (Grade School Math 8K) · pre-training data

    Via ProofPile2: Llemma Table 6 lists 30-gram hits between GSM8k test problems and OpenWebMath documents (2 test examples, 3 documents; 0 for solutions); CONDA 2024 report lists gsm8k as contaminated in EleutherAI/proof-pile-2.

    Evidence
  • HumanEval · pre-training data

    Via StarCoder data: Riddell et al. (2024) find 18.9% of HumanEval gold solutions (and 20.8% of MBPP) seen in the Python split of the Stack training data of StarCoderBase (60.40 GB searched), despite the Stack's string-matching decontamination.

    Evidence
  • MMLU (Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding) · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    DCLM's MMLU overlap rule (question last sentence + one answer option on the same page; recall-oriented, likely overestimate) flagged 0.007% of DCLM-Baseline documents (vs 0.001% Dolma-V1.7, 0.009% FineWeb-Edu). Removing the flagged overlaps did not lower MMLU accuracy at 7B-2x scale (51.8 -> 52.7).

    Evidence
  • HellaSwag · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    Overlaps between HellaSwag test examples and DCLM-Baseline pages were detected and removed for an ablation (fraction not reported in text); removal did not lower HellaSwag accuracy (77.9 -> 78.4). In the 10-gram token-overlap analysis HellaSwag is the notable exception with a performance gap between full and 'clean' subsets (Figure 12).

    Evidence
  • MMLU (Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding) · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    27.70% of (downsampled) MMLU test questions are 'dirty' in DCLM-baseline.

    Evidence
  • MMLU-Pro · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    16.20% of (downsampled) MMLU-Pro test questions are 'dirty' in DCLM-baseline.

    Evidence
  • ARC-Challenge (AI2 Reasoning Challenge, Challenge Set) · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    32.6% of ARC-Challenge test questions are 'dirty' in DCLM-baseline (17x more than in Pile-train).

Notes

Base model; card says it has not undergone alignment or safety fine-tuning. DCLM's MMLU/HellaSwag decontamination was an ablation at 7B-2x scale only; the released model was trained on non-decontaminated DCLM-Baseline (developer findings recorded on corpus dclm-baseline). DCLM-Pool is all Common Crawl data prior to 2023, but no model knowledge cutoff is stated (card: 'limited to its training data cutoff date').
