Notes

Trained on top of DeepSeek-V3-Base (pre-training data shared with deepseek-v3). The paper also cautions: 'variations of test set questions or discussions of related problems may still exist on websites that were included in the pre-training corpus' (Appendix E.2). Other reported contamination, not shown as cells: evaluation benchmarks (mathematics domain): 10-gram decontamination identified and removed about six million potential pre-training texts in the mathematics domain alone (before training)
