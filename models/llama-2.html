Contamination analyses

Analyses that found benchmark test data in this model's training data, or in a corpus it uses.

  • HellaSwag · pre-training data

    With min. match length L=40: 2,651 of 10,042 samples (26%) 'not clean' (>=20% of tokens in 10+-token n-grams found in pre-training data) and 848 (8.4%) 'dirty' (>=80%); dirty samples scored 92.2 vs 82.5 sampling mean for 70B (Z=7.42). Authors conclude HellaSwag scores appear boosted by contamination, 70B more than 7B.

    Evidence
  • MMLU (Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding) · pre-training data

    MMLU-Humanities (L=50): 709 of 4,705 samples (15%) 'not clean' (>=20% token contamination) and 520 (11%) 'dirty' (>=80%); flagged as boosted by contamination. MMLU-Overall for 70B: 2,180 of 14,042 (15.5%) not clean, 1,536 (10.9%) dirty, with a small effect on the overall score (-0.9 clean-vs-mean delta).

Notes

Contamination analysis (Appendix A.6) is on pre-training data with pretrained-only models; only HellaSwag and MMLU(-Humanities) were judged affected. Other benchmarks may have overlap without measurable benefit; the paper omits them. Chat models share pre-training data and add the listed fine-tuning data. Third-party signal on HellaSwag (indirect, not shown as a cell): Sample-specific contamination detected on HellaSwag (p < 0.5%, estimated effect 3-8%); ConStat notes the provider itself reported HellaSwag contamination.
