Notes

Paper v4 (ICML 2024) Table 1 is the data composition; arXiv v1 described an earlier mix (Google Trends, M4, Electricity, Traffic, LibCity traffic; no Wiki, synthetic or Weather). GIFT-Eval's results config for TimesFM (model_link google/timesfm-1.0-200m) sets "testdata_leakage": "Yes". Only Electricity, Traffic and Weather among the long-horizon datasets are named as training data; ETTh1/2, ETTm1/2 explicitly held out; Exchange and ILI not mentioned. Other stated exclusions, not tracked here: Monash (18 datasets without missing values, Appendix A.5.2 Table 4) (all); Darts (8 datasets, Appendix A.5.1 Table 3) (all).
