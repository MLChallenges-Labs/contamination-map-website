Notes

~1M-parameter model for anomaly detection, classification, imputation and search; three task-specialised variants (HF revisions) pre-trained on the same ~1B-point Monash + LibCity data as TTM (Ekambaram et al., 2024). '+ DRS' = additional down-sampled resolutions. No UCR/UEA or anomaly benchmark data. Splits of the pre-training datasets are not stated. The HF card list omits kaggle web traffic and NN5 (in the paper).
