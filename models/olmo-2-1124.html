Contamination analyses

Analyses that found benchmark test data in this model's training data, or in a corpus it uses.

  • MMLU (Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding) · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    DCLM's MMLU overlap rule (question last sentence + one answer option on the same page; recall-oriented, likely overestimate) flagged 0.007% of DCLM-Baseline documents (vs 0.001% Dolma-V1.7, 0.009% FineWeb-Edu). Removing the flagged overlaps did not lower MMLU accuracy at 7B-2x scale (51.8 -> 52.7).

    Evidence
  • HellaSwag · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    Overlaps between HellaSwag test examples and DCLM-Baseline pages were detected and removed for an ablation (fraction not reported in text); removal did not lower HellaSwag accuracy (77.9 -> 78.4). In the 10-gram token-overlap analysis HellaSwag is the notable exception with a performance gap between full and 'clean' subsets (Figure 12).

    Evidence
  • MMLU (Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding) · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    27.70% of (downsampled) MMLU test questions are 'dirty' in DCLM-baseline.

    Evidence
  • MMLU-Pro · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

    16.20% of (downsampled) MMLU-Pro test questions are 'dirty' in DCLM-baseline.

    Evidence
  • ARC-Challenge (AI2 Reasoning Challenge, Challenge Set) · pre-training data · in DCLM-Baseline 1.0

Notes

Instruct models (SFT, DPO, RLVR) were released with the base models and are recorded as fine_tuning. Mid-training (Dolmino) includes the GSM8K train split and GSM8K-derived synthetic data; only its FLAN component was decontaminated. The paper notes 200 of 1319 GSM8K test examples were used as a development set during mid-training data development (not as training data). Fine-tuning exclusions follow the Tulu 3 recipe the paper says it followed. Later OLMo 2 releases (OLMo-2-0325-32B, OLMo-2-0425-1B) share olmo-mix-1124 and Dolmino-mix-1124 but are separate releases with slightly different post-training (tulu-3-sft-olmo-2-mixture-0225, GRPO RLVR) and are not entered here.
