Notes

Self-supervised (x32/x128) and multi-task supervised (x64) checkpoints, all trained on the training splits of the 38 Table 7 tasks (20 forecasting tasks over 7 datasets + 18 classification datasets). Classification members: 7 UCR and 7 UEA datasets. No anomaly data in pre-training; anomaly detection is few-shot fine-tuning (see units-few-shot-anomaly-detection). Other released fine-tuned checkpoints (few-shot new-data classification/forecasting incl. ECG200, SelfRegulationSCP1, RacketSports, Handwriting, Epilepsy, StarLightCurves from Table 8; imputation) are not separate entries here.
