Notes

Not a THUML model (included in this scope; Time-300B overlaps heavily with UTSD/LOTSA sources). Paper trains Base (50M), Large (200M) and Ultra (2.4B); only 50M and 200M weights are on HF. Time-300B includes Electricity (15 min), Traffic, Traffic Hourly, Exchange Rate and CDC Fluview ILINet -- raw sources of the long-horizon ECL, Traffic, Exchange and ILI benchmarks; Time-MoE is accordingly not evaluated on ECL by Timer-XL/Sundial. Appendix text says 'eight well-established datasets' but lists six. Other stated exclusions, not tracked here: Global Temp (all).
