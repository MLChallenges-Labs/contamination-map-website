Notes

Five sizes share one recipe: 42.5% Datadog internal observability metrics (10s/60s/5m+ intervals; no customer data) + 57.5% TempoPFN synthetic; 5.04T points (313m/1B/2.5B) or 3.40T (4m/22m). No public data in pretraining, so no overlap with GIFT-Eval or long-horizon datasets is possible from the stated recipe. Disclosure marked vague because the real-data part is an undisclosed internal source (no named datasets). No BOOM leakage statement in the Toto 2.0 report.
