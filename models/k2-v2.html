Notes

70B dense; ~12T-token pre-training, four mid-training stages (TxT360-Midas, recorded as pretraining) and SFT only (no RL/preference optimisation per report). Table 1 of the report gives per-source information cut-off dates (e.g. CommonCrawl July 2024, MegaMath Nov 2024) but no model-level training cutoff is stated. Jais Arabic data is named but not shown to be published separately. Decontamination statements not shown as cells (they name no benchmark tracked here, or none at all): 12 downstream benchmarks (MegaMath decontamination; benchmarks not named in K2-V2 report); evaluation benchmarks (unspecified) - SFT data; test subsets of mid-training math problem sources. Other reported contamination, not shown as cells: aime-2025: Sentence-level memorisation rate 14.43% for K2 base; the authors say most 2025 memorisation comes from the last, formulaic sentence of each problem.
