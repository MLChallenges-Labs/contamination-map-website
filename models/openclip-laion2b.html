Notes

The list includes the ViT checkpoints most often used as AD backbones. pretrained.py also has ConvNeXt and RoBERTa-text LAION-2B checkpoints with the laion2b_* tags. disclosure is full_list because the training set is one published dataset, but that dataset is an open-ended web scrape. The scaling-law paper's pHash duplicate check (Appendix B.1) covered only ImageNet variants and VTAB and removed nothing from pre-training. training_cutoff is on the laion-2b corpus.
