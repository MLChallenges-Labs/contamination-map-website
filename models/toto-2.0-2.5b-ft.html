Notes

Benchmark-only checkpoint: Toto 2.0 2.5B finetuned 10,000 steps on GIFT-Eval Pretrain (45%), Datadog metrics (30%), GIFT-Eval train split (15%) and TempoPFN synthetic (10%). It is a finetuned (not zero-shot) GIFT-Eval submission. The companion Toto-2.0-Family-and-Friends is an XGBoost-gated ensemble of 10 foundation models (not a separately pretrained model) and is not listed as a model here.
