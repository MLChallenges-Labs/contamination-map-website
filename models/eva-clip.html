Notes

The EVA-02 MIM objective regresses features of a frozen EVA-CLIP teacher (EVA-01-CLIP-g, itself trained on LAION-400M per the EVA-CLIP README); this is feature distillation, not data use, and is not recorded as training data. No deduplication or overlap analysis in the EVA-CLIP paper. Separate entry eva-clip-e for the E/14 models, which use LAION-2B and OpenCLIP text encoders.
