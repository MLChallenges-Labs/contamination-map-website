Notes

Paper notes Molmo2 lags on reasoning benchmarks (MathVista, MMMU), 'possibly due to the lack of multi-modal reasoning training data'. A decontamination step is reported only for MOSE videos against the MeViS validation set (video tracking), not for the multimodal benchmarks here. Accepts image input: “Molmo2 is a family of open vision-language models developed by the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2) that support image, video and multi-image understanding and grounding.”
