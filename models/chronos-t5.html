Notes

Pre-trained on 10M TSMixup augmentations of the 28 real training datasets plus 1M KernelSynth series (ratio 9:1). Benchmark I (15 datasets) is in-domain: trained on all but the last H observations. Benchmark II (27 datasets, incl. ETT, Exchange Rate, Traffic, Weather) was not used for training. Electricity (15 Min./Hourly/Weekly) is in the training data (test window held out). The paper's footnote concedes Benchmark II start times are not guaranteed to be after the training data's last timestamp. Identical copies of the checkpoints exist under the autogluon/ HF org. Other stated exclusions, not tracked here: chronos-benchmark-ii (all); chronos-benchmark-i (test); Australian Electricity (all); CIF 2016 (all); Dominick (all); ERCOT Load (all); FRED-MD (all); M1 (Monthly) (all); M1 (Quarterly) (all); M1 (Yearly) (all); M3 (Monthly) (all); M3 (Quarterly) (all); M3 (Yearly) (all); M5 (all); NN5 (Daily) (all); NN5 (Weekly) (all); Tourism (Monthly) (all); Tourism (Quarterly) (all); Tourism (Yearly) (all); Weather (all).
