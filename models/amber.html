Notes

7B LLaMA-architecture model trained on 1.26T tokens; 360 intermediate checkpoints. AmberChat (SFT) and AmberSafe (DPO on AmberChat) were released within about two weeks of the base model (HF repos created 2023-11-30 to 2023-12-15), so their data is recorded here as fine_tuning. HF org LLM360 now redirects to IFM.
