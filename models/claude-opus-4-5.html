Notes

Included (point release) because its system card introduced Anthropic's documented decontamination methodology (Section 2.2: exact-substring removal, 20-gram fuzzy decontamination, canary filtering, manual inspection), which later cards reference. The card states manual inspection 'confirmed low levels of contamination for many evaluations (e.g. Humanity's Last Exam)' and that decontamination was 'significantly improved with Claude Opus 4.5'. The docs give a training data cutoff of Aug 2025 while the system card describes internet data 'up to May 2025'. Decontamination statements not shown as cells (they name no benchmark tracked here, or none at all): Terminal-Bench.
