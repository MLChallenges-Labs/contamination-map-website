Notes

Learnable object-agnostic text prompts (CLIP frozen). Trained on the MVTec AD test set (15 epochs) and used for all reported zero-shot results except MVTec AD itself (VisA, MPDD, BTAD, SDD, DAGM, DTD-Synthetic and the medical datasets). In the repo the MVTec-trained checkpoint is the folder named '9_12_4_multiscale_visa' (train.sh: base_dir ..._multiscale_visa with --dataset mvtec; test.sh uses it with --dataset visa); the naming is counter-intuitive. The paper also reports a variant fine-tuned on a medical set built from ColonDB plus the test split of Endo (Table 3); no checkpoint for it is released.
