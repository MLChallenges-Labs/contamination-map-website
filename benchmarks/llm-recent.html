Commonly reported LLM benchmarks; grouping used on this site, not an official suite. Harder reasoning, math, science and coding benchmarks typical of 2024-2026 model reports.

Test split: GPQA Diamond: all 198 questions (no train/test split). MMLU-Pro: test (12,032; the 70-question validation split supplies few-shot examples).
