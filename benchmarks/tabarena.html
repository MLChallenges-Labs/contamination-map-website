TabArena TabArena-v0.1

Living benchmark for predictive machine learning on small-to-medium IID tabular data: 51 manually curated classification and regression datasets (500-250,000 training samples), hosted as OpenML tasks (dataset ids 46904-46980, task ids 363612-363712).

HomepageLeaderboard

Test split: Outer evaluation splits are fixed OpenML tasks: 3-fold outer cross-validation (stratified by class for classification), repeated 10 times (30 splits) for datasets with fewer than 2,500 samples and 3 times (9 splits) otherwise. Models are tuned/validated with 8-fold inner cross-validation on each outer training fold. TabArena-Lite uses only the first outer split of every dataset. Every row of a dataset appears in some outer test fold, so any pre-training on the dataset overlaps test data.

Normalized error (TabArena), mean over every dataset (mean over TabArena's 9 or 30 outer splits); lower is better. From TabArena-v0.1 living leaderboard, per-dataset results (results_per_split.csv) at a6688200ae46 (Apache-2.0), retrieved 2026-10-16. Per-split results of the current TabArena-v0.1 leaderboard (TabArenaContext at repo commit a668820, 2026-10-16; 107 leaderboard entries; HF space TabArena/leaderboard is also Apache-2.0), exported with TabArena's own run_export_results_per_split.py (per-split results are not hosted; the script downloads the processed results from data.tabarena.ai) and averaged over outer splits per dataset. Fixed OpenML tasks, 3-fold outer CV x 3 or x 10 repeats, 8-fold inner bagging; metric is 1-AUC for 30 binary, log-loss for 8 multiclass and RMSE for 13 regression datasets. First metric = TabArena's per-dataset normalised error, recomputed exactly as compute_normalized_error_dynamic: (error - best)/(median - best) over all 107 leaderboard entries (incl. imputed ones), clipped to [0,1]; it depends on the roster at this commit and ties at 1.0 for entries worse than the median, so rank by 'error' within a dataset when that matters (per-entry means reproduce the leaderboard's normalized-error column). Foundation models use the '(default)' variant, i.e. the released checkpoint with TabArena's default inference settings (most have no tuned variant); baselines use the leaderboard's '(tuned + ensemble)' variant and AutoGluon 1.6 extreme (4h). Results TabArena imputed with RandomForest (TabPFNv2 and Mitra v1 on 18 datasets outside their size limits, TabICL v1.1 on 15 regression/large datasets) are omitted. TabArena's Elo/rank are aggregate, not per dataset, so not included. Real-TabPFN (v2 finetuned) and non-real TabPFN-2.5 were not evaluated stand-alone; TabICL v1 (20250208) was not evaluated. The coloured bar shows how much of this benchmark each model may have seen.

Notes

As of 2026-10-15 the repository README describes TabArena-v0.1 (51 datasets) as the IID benchmark and BeyondArena (142 datasets incl. temporal and grouped tasks, arXiv 2606.30410) as a complementary benchmark that 'will superseed TabArena-v0.1 in the future'. BeyondArena is not covered here. TabArena-v0.1 datasets are re-uploads to OpenML with new ids, so other papers usually cite older OpenML ids for the same data.
