UEA Multivariate Time Series Classification Archive (2018)

First iteration (2018 vintage) of the multivariate time-series classification archive by UEA and UCR: 30 datasets, formatted to equal length, with no missing values and with train/test splits provided (splits given by the data donors were retained). Sources include the UCI Machine Learning archive, Brain Computer Interface competitions, Kaggle competitions and datasets made by the authors. Hosted at www.timeseriesclassification.com.
