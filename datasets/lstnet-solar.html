Solar (LSTNet, 137 Alabama PV plants)

Solar power production of 137 PV plants in Alabama, 2006, every 10 minutes (52,560 steps), from NREL via LSTNet. GIFT-Eval's 'solar' dataset (used at 10T, H, D, W-FRI).

Notes

GIFT-Eval: 'We utilize the solar dataset from LSTNet'. Alias 'solar_10_minutes' is the Monash version, which Monash describes as 137 Alabama series at 10 minutes in 2006 derived from the data used by Lai et al. (2017). The bare names 'solar' / 'Solar' are ambiguous (e.g. 'Solar Power' in LOTSA is AEMO 4-second data) and are not aliases.
