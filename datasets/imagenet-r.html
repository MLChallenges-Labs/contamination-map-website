Vision / Dataset

ImageNet-R (Renditions)

30,000 renditions (art, cartoons, deviantart, graffiti, embroidery, graphics, origami, paintings, patterns, plastic and plush objects, sculptures, sketches, tattoos, toys, video games) of 200 ImageNet-1k classes. Test-only: all 30,000 images are evaluated, restricted to the 200-class subset of ImageNet labels.

Also known as
ImageNet-R, ImageNet-Renditions, ImageNet Rendition, imagenet-r, wds/imagenet-r, IN-R
Domain
object recognition (renditions)

github.com

    ImageNet-R reuses ImageNet-Sketch line drawings (reference [37] is the ImageNet-Sketch paper) for its 200 classes, so the two share images; neither is a subset of the other.

Notes

Images collected primarily from Flickr and filtered by MTurk annotators and graduate students (Section 3.1). New images for ImageNet classes, not ImageNet images, so no relation to imagenet-1k is recorded. Paper v1: 29 Jun 2020. published_from is left empty: the test images were public on the web before the dataset was released, so a release date would not bound what a web crawl could contain.
