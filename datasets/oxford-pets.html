Vision / Dataset

Oxford-IIIT Pet

7,349 images of 37 cat and dog breeds (roughly 200 per class), with breed, head bounding box and pixel-level trimap annotations. The official test split (3,669 images) is evaluated; CLIP and DataComp report mean per-class accuracy.
