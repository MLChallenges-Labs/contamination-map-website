TSB-AD-M (TSB-AD multivariate)

Multivariate part of the TSB-AD benchmark (Liu & Paparrizos, NeurIPS 2024 D&B): 200 curated, labelled multivariate time series from 17 datasets (GHL, Daphnet, Exathlon, Genesis, OPP, SMD, SWaT, PSM, SMAP, MSL, CreditCard, GECCO, MITDB, SVDB, LTDB, CATSv2, TAO). Series-level split: Eval set (180 series) and Tuning set (20 series). Within each series, the file name gives a training prefix ('tr_N') that semi-supervised detectors may train on; labels cover the whole series.

Also known as
TSB-AD-M, TSB-AD multivariate, TSB-AD-M-Eval, TSB-AD-M-Tuning
