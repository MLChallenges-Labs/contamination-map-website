Vision / Dataset

SUN397

Scene recognition benchmark: 397 well-sampled categories of the SUN database, at least 100 images per category, 108,754 images in total; the paper's protocol uses 10 partitions of 50 training and 50 test images per class. Evaluation splits differ between suites: CLIP used 19,850 test images (Table 9); CLIP_benchmark's torchvision loader (`sun397`) ignores splits and DataComp lists all 108,754 images.
