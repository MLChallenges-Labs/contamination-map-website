UCR Time Series Anomaly Archive (Hexagon ML/UCR, KDD Cup 2021)

250 univariate time series, each designed to contain exactly one anomaly, introduced by Wu & Keogh. Each file name encodes the split: the first N points (e.g. '2500' in UCR_Anomaly_BIDMC1_2500_5400_5600) are intended as training data and the last two numbers give the location of the single anomaly in the remainder. Anomalies are either natural (confirmed out-of-band) or artificially introduced; TSB-AD notes that most are introduced artificially. Released after the SIGKDD 2021 time-series anomaly detection contest (KDD Cup 2021, hosted by Hexagon ML), which used 200 series split into train and test phases.
