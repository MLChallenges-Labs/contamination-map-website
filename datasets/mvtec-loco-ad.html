Vision / Dataset

MVTec LOCO AD

3644 images from five categories (breakfast box, juice bottle, pushpins, screw bag, splicing connectors) with structural and logical anomalies: 1772 training, 304 validation and 1568 test images. Training and validation images are anomaly-free; the test set contains anomaly-free images and images with logical and structural anomalies, with pixel-precise ground truth for each anomalous region.
