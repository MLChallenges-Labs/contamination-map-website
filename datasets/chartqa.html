ChartQA

Questions about real charts crawled from Statista, Pew Research, Our World in Data and OECD (20,882 charts). Two parts: ChartQA-H with 9,608 human-written questions (AMT) and ChartQA-M with 23,111 questions generated by T5 from Statista's human-written summaries. Random train/val/test split; test = 1,250 human + 1,250 machine-generated questions (2,500), which is the evaluated split (relaxed accuracy). Test questions and answers are public (vis-nlp/ChartQA GitHub 'ChartQA Dataset/test' with test_human.json and test_augmented.json; HF copies).
