Vision / Dataset

ImageNet-Sketch

Black-and-white sketch images for the 1,000 ImageNet-1k classes, found with Google Image queries 'sketch of <class name>' and manually cleaned; nominally 50 images per class (50,000), with flipped or rotated copies filling classes that had fewer than 50 after cleaning. The released archive used by CLIP_benchmark and DataComp has 50,889 images. Test-only: the whole set is evaluated.

Also known as
ImageNet Sketch, imagenet_sketch, wds/imagenet_sketch

Notes

The paper states the images are 'collected independently from the original validation images'; they are not ImageNet images, so no relation to imagenet-1k is recorded (same class list only). Some of its line drawings were reused in ImageNet-R (see imagenet-r). Paper v1: 29 May 2019. published_from is left empty: the test images were public on the web before the dataset was released, so a release date would not bound what a web crawl could contain.
