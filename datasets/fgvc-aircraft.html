Vision / Dataset

FGVC-Aircraft

Fine-grained aircraft photographs annotated with model variant, family and manufacturer, divided into three equally sized training, validation and test subsets. Evaluation suites use the 100 model variants and the 3,333-image test split, scored with mean per-class accuracy.

Also known as
FGVC Aircraft, fgvc_aircraft, FGVCAircraft, wds/fgvc_aircraft, Fine-Grained Visual Classification of Aircraft
Domain

Notes

Photographs contributed by airplane spotters via Airliners.net. The paper describes 10,000 images of 100 variants (CLIP Table 9: 6,667 train, 3,333 test) while the home page says 10,200 images of 102 variants. Release 2013a was the first public release; test labels were withheld until the ICCV 2013 FGComp challenge and included from 2013b. Paper v1: 21 Jun 2013. published_from is left empty: the test images were public on the web before the dataset was released, so a release date would not bound what a web crawl could contain.
