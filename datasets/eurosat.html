Vision / Dataset

EuroSAT

27,000 labelled, geo-referenced Sentinel-2 satellite image patches in 10 land-use and land-cover classes (13 spectral bands; the RGB version is used for CLIP-style evaluation). No official test split: CLIP used 10,000 training and 5,000 test images (Table 9); CLIP_benchmark and DataComp use the VTAB split (`vtab/eurosat`, 5,400 test images).

Also known as
