TSB-AD-U (TSB-AD univariate)

Univariate part of the TSB-AD benchmark (Liu & Paparrizos, NeurIPS 2024 D&B): 870 curated, labelled univariate time series from 23 datasets, 12 of which are univariate channels extracted from multivariate datasets (e.g. SMD (U), MSL (U), SMAP (U), SWaT (U)); the others include UCR (anomaly archive), NAB, YAHOO, IOPS, MGAB, WSD, SED, TODS, NEK, Stock and Power. Series were filtered and labels checked or corrected (extended/segmented) by human annotators assisted by detectors. Series-level split: Eval set (350 series, with sampling to balance UCR, YAHOO and WSD) and Tuning set (48 series) for hyperparameter tuning. Within each series, the file name gives a training prefix ('tr_N') that semi-supervised detectors may train on; labels cover the whole series.
