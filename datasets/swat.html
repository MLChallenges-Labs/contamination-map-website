Secure Water Treatment (SWaT)

Data from the iTrust (SUTD) Secure Water Treatment testbed, a scaled-down six-stage water treatment plant. Main dataset SWaT.A1_Dec 2015: 11 days of continuous operation, 7 days of normal operation and 4 days with attack scenarios (41 attacks), network traffic plus the values of all 51 sensors and actuators, labelled normal/abnormal. The official page defines no train/test split beyond the normal and attack parts. Later releases (SWaT.A2 to SWaT.A9) add further normal and attack runs collected up to December 2023.
