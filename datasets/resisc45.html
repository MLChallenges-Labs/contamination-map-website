Vision / Dataset

NWPU-RESISC45

31,500 remote-sensing images in 45 scene classes, 700 per class. No official test split: CLIP used 3,150 training and 25,200 test images (Table 9); CLIP_benchmark and DataComp use the VTAB split (`vtab/resisc45`, 6,300 test images).

Also known as
RESISC45, NWPU RESISC45, vtab/resisc45, wds/vtab/resisc45
Domain
remote sensing (aerial scenes)
