Vision / Dataset

CIFAR-10

60,000 32x32 colour images in 10 mutually exclusive classes (airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck): 50,000 training and 10,000 test images (1,000 per class). The 10,000-image test set is evaluated.

Also known as
CIFAR10, vtab/cifar10, wds/vtab/cifar10, tfds/cifar10
Domain
general object recognition (low resolution)
Homepage
www.cs.toronto.edu

Notes

CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 are labelled subsets of the 80 Million Tiny Images dataset, which was assembled by searching Google, Flickr, Altavista and other search engines (tech report, Section 1.2.1). 80 Million Tiny Images is not in the registry. published_from is left empty: the test images were public on the web before the dataset was released, so a release date would not bound what a web crawl could contain.
