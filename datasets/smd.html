Server Machine Dataset (SMD)

5-week-long multivariate dataset of 28 machines (entities machine-<group>-<index>, 3 groups) from a large Internet company, 38 dimensions, released with OmniAnomaly (Su et al., KDD 2019). Each machine's series is split into two halves of equal length: train (former half, no labels) and test (latter half) with point labels (test_label) and the dimensions responsible for each anomaly (interpretation_label). 708,405 training and 708,420 test points; anomaly ratio 4.16%. Machines should be trained and tested separately.
