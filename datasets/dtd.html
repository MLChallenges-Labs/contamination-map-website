Describable Textures Dataset (DTD)

5,640 texture images in 47 categories (120 per category), split into equal training, validation and test parts of 40 images per class; 10 such splits are provided. Evaluation suites use the 1,880-image test set of one split (CLIP Table 9: 3,760 train, 1,880 test).
