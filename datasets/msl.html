Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) telemetry anomaly dataset

Real telemetry of the Curiosity rover (Mars Science Laboratory) released by NASA JPL with Telemanom (Hundman et al., KDD 2018): 27 anonymised telemetry channels, each with one telemetry value plus one-hot encoded command information (55 dimensions in the OmniAnomaly table), 36 expert-labelled anomaly sequences (from Incident Surprise, Anomaly reports). Pre-split train and test sets per channel; labels (anomaly_sequences) refer to the test set; values are pre-scaled to (-1,1) using the test-set min/max and timestamps are anonymised. OmniAnomaly statistics: 58,317 training and 73,729 test points, anomaly ratio 10.72%.
