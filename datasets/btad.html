Vision / Dataset

BTAD

Real-world industrial anomaly dataset with 2830 RGB images of 3 industrial products showing body and surface defects; products 1, 2 and 3 have 400, 1000 and 399 training images. Training uses normal images only; each anomalous image has a pixel-wise ground-truth mask.
