Vision / Dataset

Oxford Flowers-102

102 flower categories commonly occurring in the United Kingdom, 40 to 258 images each; 10 training and 10 validation images per class (2,040 in total) and 6,149 test images. The test split is evaluated with mean per-class accuracy.

Also known as
Oxford Flowers 102, 102 Category Flower Dataset, Flowers102, Flowers-102, flowers, vtab/flowers, wds/vtab/flowers
Domain
flowers (fine-grained)

Notes

Images 'mainly downloaded from the web' (ICVGIP 2008 paper). The 2008 paper reports 6,129 test images; CLIP (Table 9) and DataComp use 6,149. published_from is left empty: the test images were public on the web before the dataset was released, so a release date would not bound what a web crawl could contain.
