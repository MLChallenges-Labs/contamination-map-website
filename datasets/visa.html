Vision / Dataset

VisA

10,821 high-resolution color images (9,621 normal, 1,200 anomalous) of 12 objects (PCB1-4, Capsules, Candles, Macaroni1, Macaroni2, Cashew, Chewing gum, Fryum, Pipe fryum) with image- and pixel-level annotations. In the 1-class protocol 90% of normal images form the train set and the remaining 10% of normal images plus all anomalous images form the test set; 2-class high-/low-shot splits are also defined. The released split files reorganise the data into MVTec-AD-style train (normal) / test (normal + anomalous) / ground_truth folders.
